HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, real numbers

After the launch transaction everything runs on its own: Discord says who was a member, the chain says when the coin graduates.

01 · PASTE

Paste any Discord invite

We read the server behind it: name, icon, member count, online count. Pick a name, ticker and logo; the server icon is the default.

02 · LAUNCH

Your dev buy goes to the members

One transaction on pump.fun. 1 SOL ≈ 34.2M coins (3.42% of supply), all sent to the MEMBERS escrow, memo mem:<server id>. You keep 0.

03 · SIGN

Members log in with Discord

Counts if you were in the server before the coin launched and your account is 30+ days old. One Discord account and one wallet per coin. The owner can't sign.

04 · SPLIT

Equal split at graduation

1,000 signed at 1 SOL → 34,200 each. Nobody signs → the coins burn. The owner claims 100% of the creator fees any time.

VERIFY IT YOURSELF

Every rule is on-chain

Each launch is one transaction: create, buy, send the whole buy to the escrow, memo mem:<Discord server id>. Check any of it on Solscan.

Each coin's fee wallet

Every coin's pump.fun creator is its own fee wallet, derived from the escrow key and the mint, so nobody picks it and it can't be reused:

seed = HMAC-SHA256(escrow secret, "mem-fee:" + mint)
fee wallet = ed25519 keypair(seed)

Its fees only pay out to the Discord account that owns the server. Paste a mint to check that its pump.fun creator is that wallet:

FAQ

Questions from the server

Who gets the coins?

Every member who was in the server before the coin launched, logs in with Discord and signs with a wallet before the coin graduates. At graduation the whole dev buy splits equally between them: 1,000 signed at 1 SOL is 34,200 coins each.

Can someone farm it with alts?

An alt only counts if it was already in the server before the coin launched and the Discord account is at least 30 days old. One Discord account and one wallet per coin, so a fresh alt joining after launch gets nothing.

What does the server owner get?

100% of the creator fees. Every coin's pump.fun creator is its own MEMBERS fee wallet, and only the Discord account that owns the server can claim from it, any time. The owner can't sign for the dev buy.

What if nobody signs?

The escrow burns the whole dev buy on-chain when the coin graduates. Nobody keeps it, not the launcher and not MEMBERS.

What if I leave the server?

Your signature still counts. What matters is that you were a member before the coin launched and signed before it graduated.

What does the launcher get?

Nothing from the dev buy: every coin of it goes to the members' escrow in the same transaction. You launch it because you want the coin to exist, then buy like everyone else.

What does MEMBERS see from my Discord?

Your account id, name and age, the servers you're in and when you joined the coin's server. We keep a hash of your id so one account signs once; your name shows masked, like ka•••r.

When does signing close?

The moment the coin graduates on pump.fun. The count is fixed then, so the split can't move after.
